Cladding
Charcoal standing-seam metal, matte finish, concealed fastener. Vertical seam at 16" o.c. Matte matters — a gloss panel throws the bay-door glow back at the camera and kills the dusk shot.
Glazing
Full-lite aluminum sectional doors, 14' × 14', clear anodized frame with low-iron glass. Client wing is a storefront system, floor to 12'.
Floor
100% solids epoxy with gray flake broadcast and a gloss topcoat. Koa's silhouette inlaid at each bay mouth in Signal Blue. Trench drain in bay 04 only.
Shop lighting
5000K linear LED at 90+ CRI, 100 fc at the floor. Correction bay adds four rolling towers at 6500K for defect hunting.
Client lighting
2700K throughout the lounge and office — the warm/cool split between the wings is the whole exterior composition after dark.
Millwork
Rift white oak, ebonized, with integrated LED reveals. Backlit Koa mark on the office feature wall and behind reception.
Paving
Asphalt on the client side, compacted #57 gravel on the crew side. The material change does the wayfinding without a single sign.

Identity applications

Signage &
fleet

Handy Hunks runs an orange mark on white trucks. Koa runs the inverse — a black rig with the mark reading in Signal Blue and white, so the fleet is unmistakable at 60 mph and still clean parked in a Middleburg driveway.

Building sign
12' wide full-color logo on a raised aluminum panel with a concealed sky-blue LED halo. Panel is one shade lighter than the cladding so the mark separates at night.
Monument
6' × 4' internally lit cabinet on a charcoal masonry base at the Rt. 15 entrance. Logo only — no phone number, no service list.
Bay numerals
Signal Blue vinyl numerals 01–04 on the door headers, 10" cap height, JetBrains Mono. Reads in the dusk render and in every job photo.
Fleet — trucks
Gloss black wrap. Signal Blue angular sweep from the front door through the bed, KOA in white on the door, full logo on the tailgate, phone number in white on the rear quarter only.
Fleet — trailers
Full logo lockup centered on both flanks at 7' wide, Signal Blue band above, black below. The trailer is the billboard; the truck stays restrained.
Cove light
Continuous sky-blue LED under the roof fascia, full building width. One gesture, always on after dark — it's the thing people describe when they give directions.
One rule for every application: the mark never sits on a light background outdoors. Charcoal, navy, or black only. On white it loses the glow that makes it read as Koa.
