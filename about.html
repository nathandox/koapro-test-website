The Founder

Hey, I'm Nathan.

I'm a lifelong car enthusiast based in Raleigh, NC, and I started Koa Pro Detail because I genuinely believed there was a better way to get your car detailed — no drop-offs, no waiting rooms, just pro-level results brought directly to your driveway.

Growing up, I was always the person in the neighborhood who kept his car immaculate. That obsession turned into a craft, and eventually a business. I invested in professional-grade equipment, trained on the best products in the industry, and built a service I'm truly proud of.

Every vehicle I touch gets treated like it's my own. Whether it's a daily driver or a show car, I bring the same attention to detail, premium products, and passion for perfection to every single appointment.
