Why Routine Maintenance Matters.

Your car doesn't lose its shine overnight. Over time it collects brake dust, water spots, UV damage, bonded contaminants, interior stains, leather wear, and paint oxidation.

By the time most owners notice, fixing it can cost hundreds or even thousands of dollars. The Koa Care Plan stops those problems before they start and protects your car's long-term value.

Protecting your car is always cheaper than restoring it.

Your vehicle is one of your biggest investments. Keep it protected, hold its value, and enjoy it looking its best every day of the year.

  • Lower restoration costs
